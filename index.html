What this is

We went through Audiomack the way a new listener does, then rebuilt the screen where Plus is decided.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion across the market, not inside one app. We went through your iOS onboarding, from “New Music Changes Everything” to “What is your age?” and “What do you want to listen to?”, on to the paywall right after it and the Plus screen behind “Get Plus”, and checked the price against your App Store listing.

It is an honest paywall: “Audiomack is FREE”, a clear price and a real way out. What it does not do is use what Audiomack just learned. A listener picks Burna Boy, Asake and Tems; the next screen shows five other faces and talks only about ads. Unlimited downloads, the promise in your App Store name “Play Music Offline”, sit on a different screen. The 7 free days appear only on the button, and choosing ads ends the conversation.

We did not redesign your paywall

The artist bubbles over the contour lines, “Audiomack is FREE” in white and orange, the orange trial pill, the outlined ad-supported pill and the footer row all stay. Only the faces, the words, the order and one small sheet change.

Paywall rebuild · October 2026

The Audiomack+ paywall, finishing what the onboarding started

Same screen, same art direction, same price. Five changes, each fed by something Audiomack already asks, already sells or already publishes. Flip Today above the phone to see the screen as it ships.

1

Their artists on the paywall

Today
The same five faces and Audiomack is FREE for everyone, one screen after they told you who they want to hear.
Now
Uses: the artists from “What do you want to listen to?”, in the order picked, with the name pills from that same picker. “Top artists in your area”, from your welcome screen, fill any empty bubble.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +15–20%to trial
2

What they reached for, first

Today
This paywall talks only about ads: But if you don’t want ads, we do have a subscription. The four benefits sit on the Plus screen, in one fixed order.
Now
Uses: what opened Plus (the end of onboarding, the home banner “Get Unlimited Downloads · Unlock”, or a Plus-only track) and the four benefit lines and icons from your Plus screen, word for word.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +10–15%to trial
3

The trial, spelled out

Today
We do have a subscription for 6,99 €/month. The 7 free days appear only on the button; Cancel anytime. sits at the bottom.
Now
A card in the Plus screen’s bordered style: 7 days free, then 6,99 €/month, then Today 0 € · Day 7 6,99 €/month starts · Before day 7 cancel and you are not charged.
Uses: Premium Monthly, 7 days free then 6,99 €/month, from your paywall, and You won’t be charged until your free trial ends. Cancel anytime. from your Plus screen.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +10–15%to trial
4

Your rating, under the button

Today
Nothing between the two buttons says how many people already listen here.
Now
Uses: 4.8 from 2M ratings on your US App Store listing; the Spanish screen uses 4.8 from 25K ratings on your Spanish listing.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +10–15%to trial
5

A month free when they choose ads

Today
Continue for free on ad-supported goes straight to the app. No second offer.
Now
Uses: the 30-day free trial you already run on audiomack.com/plus, as an App Store promotional offer on the same Premium Monthly. Same 6,99 €/month after. No discount, no countdown.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataARPU +10–15%from people who would have left
